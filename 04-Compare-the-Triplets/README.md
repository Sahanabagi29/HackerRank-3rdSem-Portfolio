\# Compare the Triplets



\## Problem

Compare the scores of Alice and Bob in three categories and calculate their points.



\## Approach

\- Initialize Alice's and Bob's scores to 0.

\- Compare their scores in each of the three categories.

\- If Alice's score is greater, increase Alice's points.

\- If Bob's score is greater, increase Bob's points.

\- If both scores are equal, no points are awarded.

\- Display the final scores.



\## Complexity

\- Time Complexity: O(1)

\- Space Complexity: O(1)
